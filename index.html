Unity · C# · Educational Game

Deutsch Meister

A German-language vocabulary game built in Unity with two distinct modes. In Game Mode, balloons labelled with German words fall from the sky — players must pop the balloon matching the picture shown, racing against lives and a timer that starts only after the first mistake. In Learn Mode, a four-choice quiz enforces comprehension before advancing. A built-in Vocabulary Library of nouns, adjectives, and verbs with English translations and emoji cues prepares players before they compete.

Key Technical Challenges Solved
Simultaneous balloon pops — A shared waveCompleted flag in GameManager ensures only the first click per wave registers. All subsequent inputs are discarded until the next wave begins, eliminating race conditions.
Balloon lifecycle tracking — Each balloon maintains its own isHandled boolean and reports to GameManager whether it was clicked or escaped off-screen, keeping the active-balloon counter accurate at all times.
Difficulty scaling — SpawnManager spawns 2 / 4 / 6 balloons on Easy / Medium / Hard. A random correct-index guarantees exactly one correct answer per wave; wrong words are drawn without replacement to prevent duplicating the target word.
Cross-scene persistent audio — A singleton AudioManager with DontDestroyOnLoad subscribes to scene-load events and switches music tracks contextually without audio gaps or duplicate instances.
Learn mode answer gating — The Next button is disabled by default and only becomes interactable after the correct answer, enforcing comprehension. Wrong answers trigger audio feedback without advancing the quiz.

Key Technical Challenges Solved
Ethical Lab Release Logic — Implemented a 48-hour "Management Review" holding period. This prevents patients from receiving critical diagnoses (e.g., life-altering findings) via notification before a doctor can review and provide counsel.
Safety-Critical Prescribing — Built a real-time allergy cross-reference system. If a doctor prescribes a drug (e.g., Penicillin) matching the patient's documented allergy list, the system triggers a mandatory UI block requiring explicit risk acknowledgement.
Minor & Elderly Privacy (Consent Rules) — Solved complex account inheritance. Designed permissions where parents have full record access for children < 14, but automatically switches to "Schedule-Only" for ages 14-17 to respect legal minor privacy rights.
No-Show & Slot Optimization — To reduce clinic gaps, the system enforces a 24-hour online cancellation block and automatically bans online booking for users with $\geq 3$ no-shows, requiring manual front-desk intervention.
Clinical Sync Workflow — Designed a high-speed handoff from Nurse triage to Doctor charts. Vitals (BP, HR, Temp) recorded by nurses appear instantly as pinned alerts in the Doctor's dashboard to minimize redundant questioning and lag.
